1. FIP (FIELDING INDEPENDENT PITCHING)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FIP is more accurate than ERA because
it removes defense luck.
Formula: FIP = ((13×HR + 3×BB - 2×K) 
/ IP) + 3.10

Fetch from MLB Stats API:
https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/people/
{pitcherId}/stats?stats=season
&group=pitching&season=2026

Extract: homeRuns, baseOnBalls, 
strikeOuts, inningsPitched

Calculate FIP for each pitcher.

Show both ERA and FIP:
"🔵 Gerrit Cole
   Promedio carreras (ERA): 1.89 élite 🌟
   Rendimiento real (FIP): 2.10 élite 🌟"

Use FIP tiers same as ERA:
< 2.00 → élite 🌟
2.00-2.75 → dominante 🔥
2.75-3.50 → sólido ✅
3.50-4.25 → promedio ⚪
4.25-5.00 → débil ⚠️
> 5.00 → vulnerable 🔴

When ERA and FIP differ significantly:
- FIP much worse than ERA (1.0+):
  Add "⚠️ Ha tenido suerte — 
  rendimiento real es peor"
- FIP much better than ERA (1.0+):
  Add "📈 Ha sido víctima de mala 
  suerte — es mejor de lo que parece"

Use FIP (not ERA) for all pitcher
intelligence rules (dominant/weak).

2. MATCHUP ZURDO/DERECHO (L/R)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Fetch pitcher handedness from:
https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/people/
{pitcherId}

Fetch team batting splits vs L/R from:
https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/teams/
{teamId}/stats?stats=vsLeft&vsRight
&group=hitting&season=2026

Show matchup advantage:
"⚔ Matchup: Cole (diestro) vs 
   lineup Red Sox
   Red Sox vs pitchers diestros:
   Promedio de bateo: .218 (débil)
   → Ventaja para el pitcher ✅"

OR:
"⚔️ Matchup: Sale (zurdo) vs
   lineup Yankees
   Yankees vs pitchers zurdos:
   Promedio de bateo: .271 (fuerte)
   → Ventaja para los bateadores ⚠️"

Use this to adjust projected total:
- Lineup weak vs pitcher handedness
  → reduce projected runs by 0.4
- Lineup strong vs pitcher handedness
  → increase projected runs by 0.4

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3. HEAD-TO-HEAD (H2H)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Fetch last 5 meetings between teams:
https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/schedule
?sportId=1&teamId={team1}
&opponentId={team2}&season=2026
&gameType=R

Calculate:
- Average total runs in last 5 H2H
- Over/Under record in H2H
- Home team record in H2H

Show in alert:
"📊 Últimos 5 enfrentamientos:
   Promedio de carreras: 8.2 por juego
   Over/Under: Over ganó 3 de 5
   
   Yankees en casa vs Red Sox: 
   3 ganados, 2 perdidos"

Use H2H average to weight the 
projected total:
Final projection = 70% model + 
                   30% H2H average

If H2H strongly contradicts model
(difference > 2 runs):
Add: "⚠️ Historial sugiere diferente
— considerar antes de apostar"
