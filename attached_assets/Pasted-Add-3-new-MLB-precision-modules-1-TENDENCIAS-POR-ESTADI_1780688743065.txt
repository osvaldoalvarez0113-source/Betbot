Fetch team bullpen ERA from:
https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/teams/
{teamId}/stats?stats=season
&group=pitching&season=2026

Calculate bullpen ERA separately
from starter ERA.

Show in alert:
"⚾ Bullpen Yankees:
   ERA promedio: 3.45 sólido ✅"

Use bullpen data when:
- Starter has high pitch count history
  (>100 pitches avg → likely exits 
   by inning 6)
- Late innings matter for the total

Weighted projection:
- Innings 1-6: use starter ERA
- Innings 7-9: use bullpen ERA
- Final projection = weighted average

If bullpen ERA > 5.00:
Add: "⚠️ Bullpen vulnerable —
     carreras tardías esperadas →
     considera Over en juegos cerrados"

  "⚠️ Cole con 8+ días sin lanzar
   → Puede estar oxidado
   → Añade 0.3 carreras al total"
  Adjust: +0.3 runs to projection
